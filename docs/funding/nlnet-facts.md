## Problem and numbers

- Portability friction · provider-owned memories and instructions sit in separate account surfaces. Moving context can mean exporting, prompting for a summary and pasting it into a different service. An import copies information into the receiving provider’s system. Official migration features already exist. [ChatGPT Memory FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-faq), [Claude memory import and export](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12123587-import-and-export-your-memory-from-claude), [Gemini import](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16868299?hl=en).
- Adoption context · 49% of U.S. adults report using AI chatbots in Pew’s February 2026 survey of 5,119 adults. Reported use by product includes 44% for ChatGPT and 24% for Gemini. In the same survey, 20% of U.S. adults say they use chatbots for medical advice. These product shares overlap by an unknown amount in this comparison. [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/).
- Everyday use · a 2025 OpenAI co-authored working paper puts non-work activity above 70% of consumer ChatGPT messages by July 2025. The paper studies ChatGPT and supplies adoption and use context. [How People Use ChatGPT](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf).
- Evidence gaps · no sourced estimate here for how often people repeat preferences, time lost, cross-provider user counts or bleib demand. Pilot measurements to collect include repeated facts per task, time and actions to set up, and save dismissals or incorrect matches, using synthetic or participant-approved examples.

## Technical challenges

- Three providers change authentication, chat composers and memory pages independently. Preserve the draft through asynchronous changes and pause on an unknown layout. Synthetic fixtures and separate live test accounts exercise different parts of that problem.
- Local relevance is deterministic and deliberately narrow. Keep uncertain text in review, test cross-topic matches and enforce private-kind decisions across old grants, revocation and upgrades.
- Browser identity, destination and consent must be bound to the actual tab and request. Keep the pairing credential off provider pages. Keep policy and receipt checks ahead of release. Treat imported HTML-like text as data.
- Preserve recoverability without exposing keys. Account for platform keyring behavior, old encrypted data, partial writes, portable backups and installer upgrades.
- Specify interoperable memory data and migrations. Check format round trips and use fixtures for parsing and compatibility. Distinguish exact-text receipt evidence from any claim that a provider retained or acted on it.
